*Last updated: May 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how BioFlowHub ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit **bioflowhub.com** (the "Site").

By using this Site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

**Automatically collected data**

When you visit BioFlowHub, certain information is collected automatically through third-party services, including:

- Browser type and version
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Referring URL (the page that brought you to our site)
- General geographic location (country or city level, not precise location)
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This data is collected through **Google Analytics** and is used solely to understand how visitors interact with the Site in order to improve content and user experience. No personally identifiable information is collected through Google Analytics.

**Information you provide voluntarily**

If you contact us via email or leave a comment through the Utterances commenting system (which uses your GitHub account), you are voluntarily providing your contact information. We use this only to respond to your message and do not share it with third parties.

## 2. Cookies

BioFlowHub uses cookies through Google Analytics to track aggregate usage patterns. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to read content on this Site.

If you interact with the Utterances comment system, GitHub may also set cookies in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Affiliate links are always disclosed clearly at the point of use. We only recommend products we believe are genuinely worth your attention.
