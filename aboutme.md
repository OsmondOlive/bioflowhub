Hi, I'm **Leonardo Olivieri Carvalho** — a biomedical scientist with a passion for translating complex health science into practical, everyday guidance.

I created **BioFlowHub** because I kept seeing a gap: most health content online is either too simplified (and misleading) or too technical (and unreadable). My goal is to sit right in the middle — evidence-based, honest, and actually useful.

### What I write about

- **Nutrition & metabolism** — what the science actually says, beyond fads
- **Sleep & recovery** — arguably the most underrated health intervention
- **Stress & the nervous system** — understanding your body's response
- **Fitness & movement** — evidence-based approaches, no hype
- **Biomedical insights** — what your lab results mean, how your body works

### Why trust what I write?

I don't have a blog because I want to sell you things. I have a blog because I spent years in biomedical sciences and realized most people never get access to what I learned in university — so I'm putting it here, for free.

When I do recommend products, I'll always be transparent about why and whether I earn a commission (see my [Recommended Products](/recommended) page).
