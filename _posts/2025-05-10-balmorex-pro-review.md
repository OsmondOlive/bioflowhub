## What to Keep in Mind

As a biomedical scientist, I always want to be honest about what topical products can and cannot do:

- Topical creams work best for **localized, surface-level discomfort** — they're less effective for deep joint issues or systemic inflammation
- They are a **complementary tool**, not a cure for structural problems like arthritis or herniated discs
- Results vary considerably between individuals depending on the cause of their pain
- Always consult a healthcare provider if pain is severe, persistent, or accompanied by swelling/redness
